About us

PT. Dimensi Inovasi Nusantara (DIN) delivers integrated engineering solutions for modern buildings. Our expertise spans civil works, mechanical and electrical systems, plumbing, automation, technical consultancy, and operation and maintenance.

Every solution is designed around the needs of commercial, industrial, and institutional facilities. All services apply to equipment and systems in medium to large-scale commercial and high-rise buildings.
